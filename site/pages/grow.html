      Keep it close. Take it with you.Offline / export
      Preparing this workspace for offline reopening…
      Application records live in IndexedDB on this browser and origin. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; an export is your portable copy. Import and starting fresh preserve the existing workspace. Each workspace has its own URL; bookmark it or use Back to return.
      Offline readiness covers this workspace and local rules. Other site pages need a connection. AI also needs its model cached and a compatible GPU; a downloaded model is not a promise of offline inference.
